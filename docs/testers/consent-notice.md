# Mile Log detection test: tester information and consent

**Who is running this:** [owner's name / company name], contact [email].

**What the test is for:** to measure whether the Mile Log test app detects your drives and measures their distance accurately, compared with your car's odometer.

## What is collected

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Your drives: GPS route, start and end times, distance | to compare the app's miles with your odometer | stored on your phone; sent to the test organiser only when you tap "Send test data" |
| The app's tracking log, plus Apple MetricKit battery and performance data | to find missed trips and measure battery use | the same: sent only when you tap "Send test data" |
| Odometer photos, and your "driving / passenger / other car" tags | the reference the app is measured against | sent by you, using the method in the instructions |
| Your Apple ID email | to invite you to TestFlight | held by the organiser and Apple |

No data is sold or shared with anyone else. Apple processes TestFlight crash reports under its own privacy policy.

## How long it's kept

All your test data is deleted **within 90 days** after the go/no-go decision (expected in early January 2027). The only exception is drives you agree, below, to let us keep in anonymised form: start and end points removed, and times shifted. These help test future versions.

## Your rights

You can stop at any time by deleting the app and telling the organiser. On request, we delete your data straight away. You can ask what we hold about you. Questions or complaints: [email]. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Safety

**Never use your phone while driving.** Take odometer photos and tap tags only when parked with the engine off.
