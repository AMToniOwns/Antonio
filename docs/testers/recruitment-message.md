# Tester recruitment message

Send this to friends, contacts, local trade groups and sole-trader forums. Edit the parts in [brackets].

---

**Subject: Could you help test a mileage app for 3 weeks?**

Hi [name],

I'm building an iPhone app that logs business mileage automatically for sole traders, so you never have to write journeys down again. Before launch I need to check that it catches every drive and measures the miles accurately. That's where I need help.

**What it involves (about 2 minutes a day, for 3–4 weeks from early December):**
- Install a test version of the app through Apple's TestFlight. It takes 2 minutes and needs an iPhone on iOS 18 or later.
- Take a photo of your car's mileage reading at the start and end of each driving day.
- When you switch off the engine, tap one button in the app to say whether you were driving, a passenger, or in someone else's car.
- Carry on driving as normal. You don't need to drive more than usual.

**I'd especially love to hear from you if you drive for work:** trades, deliveries, mobile services, visiting clients.

**Your privacy:** the test version records your drives (routes and times) so I can check its accuracy. Only I will see this data. It will be deleted within 90 days of the test ending, unless you specifically agree to let me keep an anonymised drive to test future versions. There's a one-page consent form before you start.

**What you get:** a free year of the full app when it launches, and my genuine thanks.

If you're up for it, reply with the email address you use for your Apple ID, and tell me whether you drive for work. I'll send the details by [25 November].

Thanks,
[your name]
